Sounds used in Turtle Runaway
=============================

All sounds are free and released under CC0 (public domain): they can be used without
permission or attribution. They are credited here anyway, with thanks to the authors.
For the game they were converted to 22050 Hz mono WAV, trimmed, looped and levelled.

Music
-----
music_title.wav   "Flowerbed Fields [Loop]" by Zane Little Music (first half, looped)
                  https://opengameart.org/content/flowerbed-fields-loop
music_play.wav    "Puppy Playing in The Garden" by spring (first 24 bars)
music_hurry.wav   the same song, first 8 bars played 25% faster
                  https://opengameart.org/content/puppy-playing-in-the-garden
music_end.wav     "8-Bit Victory Loop" by wolfgang
                  https://opengameart.org/content/8-bit-victory-loop
fanfare.wav       "Victory" by celestialghost8
                  https://opengameart.org/content/victory
